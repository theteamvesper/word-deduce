Privacy Policy

WORD DEDUCE — Team Vesper

Effective Date: August 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to WORD DEDUCE, operated by Team Vesper ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users ("you").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application WORD DEDUCE (available on Google Play Store and Apple App Store).

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information required to provide smooth gameplay, store your puzzle progress, and deliver reward features:

  • Account & Profile Information: Username, display name, level progress, coins earned, and game statistics. If you sign in via Google or Email, we store your email address and unique Auth ID. If you play as a Guest, an anonymous user identifier is generated.
  • Device & Usage Data: Operating system version, device model, app launch logs, and general gameplay metrics (puzzles solved, win rate, streak count).
  • Advertising & Analytics Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) and Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) used by Google AdMob to deliver rewarded video ads and app monetization.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To enable core game features, save level progression, and maintain leaderboards.
  • To synchronize game profile data securely across devices via Cloud Firestore.
  • To deliver rewarded video advertisements (AdMob) so users can earn extra hints or tries.
  • To schedule daily challenge reminder notifications on your device.
  • To detect, prevent, and troubleshoot technical issues or cheating.

4. Third-Party Services & Libraries

Our app integrates with trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics)
  • Google AdMob (Advertising & Rewarded Ads)

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your WORD DEDUCE account and associated data using either of the following methods:

  • Delete from the app: Go to Settings, scroll down, and select Delete Account. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the deletion.
  • Request deletion by email: Send an email to contact@teamvesper.com with the subject "Delete my WORD DEDUCE account." Your account and associated data will be deleted within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

WORD DEDUCE is designed for general audiences and word puzzle enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

7. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". You are advised to review this policy periodically.
